MediPass

Does this medicine work for me, and is it safe? A traffic light in seconds.

Scan the package

Hold your phone over the barcode, or take a photo of the box. MediPass shows what is good and what to watch.

The photo is sent to an AI model once, to read the name on the box, and is not stored. Barcodes are read on your device. You always confirm what was read before a traffic light is shown.
